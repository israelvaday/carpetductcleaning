FAQ

Drape & Curtain Cleaning questions

How often should I clean my drapes and curtains?

It's recommended to clean drapes and curtains every 3 to 6 months to remove dust, allergens, and odors. However, in households with pets, smokers, or high foot traffic, more frequent cleaning may be necessary. Regular maintenance helps prolong the life of your window treatments and maintains indoor air quality.

Can I clean my curtains at home?

Machine-Washable Curtains : Most synthetic fabrics like polyester and nylon can be machine-washed on a gentle cycle with cold water and mild detergent. What should I avoid when cleaning drapes and curtains?

Avoid Bleach : Bleach can weaken fabric fibers and cause discoloration. Avoid Hot Water : Hot water can shrink or distort fabrics; always use cold or lukewarm water. Avoid Overloading : Washing too many curtains at once can lead to uneven cleaning and potential damage. Avoid Harsh Chemicals : Use mild detergents and avoid fabric softeners, which can leave residues.

How can I remove wrinkles from my curtains?

Ironing : Use an iron on the appropriate setting for the fabric, placing a pressing cloth between the iron and the curtain to prevent direct heat contact.
