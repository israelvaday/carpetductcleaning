Common questions

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Irvine, CA?

Carpet cleaning in Irvine typically costs $35 to $65 per room or $150 to $300 for a whole home depending on size and condition. Carpet & Duct Cleaning provides an exact itemized quote on-site before any work begins with zero hidden charges.

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry in Irvine?

Most carpets dry within 4 to 8 hours after professional hot-water extraction in Irvine's climate. Keeping your HVAC fan on Auto and opening windows after the service speeds up drying significantly.

Is professional carpet cleaning safe for kids and pets?

Carpet & Duct Cleaning uses EPA Safer Choice certified, residue-free solutions with no harsh chemicals. All products are fully safe for children, pets, and allergy sufferers once dry.

How often should carpets be professionally cleaned in Irvine, CA?

The IICRC recommends professional carpet cleaning every 6 to 12 months for average households. Homes with pets, young children, or allergy sufferers in neighborhoods like Woodbury, Northwood, and Turtle Rock benefit from cleaning every 3 to 6 months.
