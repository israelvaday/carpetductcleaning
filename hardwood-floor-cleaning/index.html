FAQ

Hardwood Floor Cleaning questions

How often should I clean my hardwood floors?

Sweep or dust mop daily to remove dirt and debris. Vacuum weekly using a vacuum with a bare floor setting to prevent scratches. Damp mop with a microfiber mop and a pH-neutral wood cleaner every 1–2 weeks.

Can I clean hardwood floors myself?

Avoid excessive water as it can cause warping and swelling. Do not use steam mops on hardwood floors; the heat and moisture can damage the wood. Use a microfiber mop dampened with a wood-specific cleaner. Avoid harsh chemicals like bleach, ammonia, or vinegar, which can strip the finish and dull the surface.

How long does professional hardwood floor cleaning take?

Most professional hardwood floor cleaning appointments take about 1 to 2 hours , depending on the size and condition of the area. Larger jobs or additional polishing may take a bit longer, but professionals will keep you informed about timing.

Should I seal my hardwood floors after cleaning?

Prevents moisture damage by creating a barrier. Enhances durability against scratches and stains. Maintains appearance by preserving the finish.

How can I maintain the shine of my hardwood floors?

Use furniture pads to prevent scratches. Place rugs in high-traffic areas. Clean spills immediately to prevent staining. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading. Re-finish every 3–5 years to restore the floor's shine and protect the wood.
