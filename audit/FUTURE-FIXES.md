Updated 2026-10-15. Not started. Do not build until this plan is approved.

The whole site is for an older reader. Larger type, larger buttons, stronger contrast, short sentences, and the phone number easy to see. One site, every page.

## On every page

- One gold call button. Same words, same size, in the top bar, the sticky bar, and the page.
- Google Guaranteed and BBB badges larger, especially on desktop.
- Hours in the front-page navigation bar, days grouped, from the Google profile: Mon–Thu and Sun, open 24 hours. Fri, 8 AM–5 PM. Sat, closed.

- Carpet: Carpet cleaning, Pet stains, Coffee and wine stains, Carpet protection

- Drapes: Drapes and curtains, Drape pickup
- Furniture: Upholstery, Mattress cleaning
