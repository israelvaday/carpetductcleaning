What to expect

Rug Pickup & Drop-Off done right

Our IICRC-certified technicians carefully inspect each rug to determine the best cleaning approach. We use specialized equipment and EPA Safer Choice products, ensuring effective dirt and allergen removal. This method is safe for all members of your household, including children and pets.

The cleaning process involves thorough dusting, pre-treatment of spots, and gentle washing. We then rinse and dry your rug in a controlled environment to prevent shrinkage or damage. This meticulous attention to detail helps restore your rug's appearance and prolong its life.

We provide convenient pickup and drop-off services throughout Orange County, CA. This saves you the hassle of transporting large or delicate rugs. Simply schedule a time, and we will take care of the rest, returning your rug fresh and clean.

This service is ideal for busy homeowners and those with valuable or oversized rugs. We have been serving the Irvine and greater Orange County area since 2013. We strive for reliable service that fits your schedule, offering same-day and next-day appointments when available.
