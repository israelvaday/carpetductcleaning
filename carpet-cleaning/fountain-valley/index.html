How often should carpets in Fountain Valley be professionally cleaned?

Given Fountain Valley's environment, we generally recommend professional carpet cleaning every 6-12 months. Homes with pets, children, or allergy sufferers might benefit from more frequent service to manage indoor air quality.

Are your cleaning products safe for my family and pets in Fountain Valley?

Yes, we use EPA Safer Choice products for all our carpet cleaning services. These solutions are formulated to be effective yet safe for use around children and pets, which is important for Fountain Valley families.

Can you remove tough stains from my carpet in Fountain Valley?

Our truck-mounted hot-water extraction system is effective at tackling many types of carpet stains. Our IICRC-certified technicians assess each stain to determine the best approach for removal, restoring the look of your carpets.
