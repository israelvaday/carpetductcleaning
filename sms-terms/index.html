Carpet And Duct Cleaning sends text messages to customers who give us a mobile number and agree to be contacted about their quote or appointment.

What we send

Quote follow-ups, appointment confirmations and reminders, arrival updates, and post-service check-ins. Messages may be sent by an automated system.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your booking, typically a few messages per job. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts; we will send one confirmation and then no more. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (949) 992-3299. U.S. mobile numbers only. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
