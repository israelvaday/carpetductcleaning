What to expect

Leather Furniture Cleaning done right

IICRC-certified technicians are trained in the specific methods required for various types of leather. We meticulously inspect your furniture to identify the leather type and any specific concerns. This ensures a tailored and effective cleaning process.

We use gentle, pH-balanced leather cleaners that effectively remove dirt and oils without causing damage. Our process includes conditioning the leather to replenish moisture and prevent cracking. This helps maintain the leather's natural luster and softness.

The cleaning removes accumulated grime and body oils that can dull the appearance of leather over time. We focus on enhancing the look and feel of your furniture, bringing back its original richness. Your leather will feel revitalized and smooth.

This service is for homeowners in Irvine and Orange County who own leather sofas, chairs, or ottomans. We pride ourselves on attention to detail and offer convenient scheduling options. Our company has been providing quality service since 2013.

How often should I clean my leather furniture?

Fjords -+1 Conditioning : Apply a leather conditioner every 6 to 12 months to keep the leather supple and prevent cracking.

What household products can I use to clean leather furniture?

Mild Soap Solution : Mix a few drops of mild, natural soap (like dish soap) with distilled water. Martha Stewart White Vinegar Solution : Combine equal parts white vinegar and distilled water.

How can I remove stains from leather furniture?

Grease or Oil Stains : Sprinkle cornstarch or baking soda on the stain, let it sit for several hours, then vacuum it off. The Spruce Ink Stains : Dab the stain with a cotton swab dipped in rubbing alcohol. Be cautious, as this can sometimes lighten the leather's color. The Washington Post General Stains : Use a mild soap solution to gently clean the area, then dry with a clean cloth.

What should I avoid when cleaning leather furniture?

Avoid Harsh Chemicals : Do not use bleach, ammonia, or other strong chemicals. Avoid Excessive Water : Do not soak the leather; use a damp cloth instead of a wet one. Limassolian Leathers Avoid Direct Heat : Keep leather furniture away from direct heat sources like radiators or fireplaces.
